Date: 2026-08-14 (JST)
Host: MacBookPro15,2, Intel Core i5-8259U, Intel Iris Plus Graphics 655, 16 GB
Display: built-in Retina display
Power: AC power during the formal runs
Internal resolution: 1x (the 4x ceiling was not exercised; 16x was not used)

## Fixed scene and binary

The fixed scene is the Japanese AMHJ real ROM and its matching F2 slot 2
savestate. The earlier frozen-screen trial used an AMHP ROM with the Japanese
state and is rejected as an invalid ROM/state pair. With the matching pair, the
camera/HUD/time advanced after load; no ARM9 abort, `DEVICE_LOST`, `SIGABRT`,
or VUID was observed.

## Runs

Each run used the same binary, ROM, F2 state, 1x setting and VSync mode. Runs
were randomized by mode, ran for 190 seconds, discarded 600 warm-up samples,
and retained more than 10,000 measured samples. The order was
M2-R1, M0-R1, M1-R1, M1-R2, M2-R2, M0-R2, M1-R3, M0-R3, M2-R3.

| Mode | Runs | Measured samples/run | FPS mean range | Frame P50 median | Frame P95 median | Frame P99 median | Pipeline P50 median | Pipeline P95 median |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| M0 TelemetryOnly | 3 | 10,568–10,613 | 58.629–59.743 | 16.671 ms | 20.719 ms | 23.036 ms | 16.611 ms | 18.703 ms |
| M1 PresentWait | 3 | 10,319–10,483 | 58.434–59.430 | 16.678 ms | 20.125 ms | 22.442 ms | 15.851 ms | 18.425 ms |
| M2 JustInTime | 3 | 10,387–10,439 | 58.743–59.264 | 16.688 ms | 20.506 ms | 23.326 ms | 15.543 ms | 18.474 ms |

The per-run aggregate was generated by
`tools/perf/aggregate-vulkan-latency.py --warmup 600`. Every run had zero
invalid rows, zero timing queue-full events and swapchain generation 2 with no
measured swapchain recreation. M2 reported the GOOGLE backend and absolute
targets for 100% of measured rows. M1/M2 bounded-wait timeout rates were
approximately 54.4–58.5%, so those modes are not suitable for this low-spec
machine's shipping default.

## Decision

M0 remains the shipping default. M2 reduced the median pipeline P50 by about
1.068 ms versus M0, but its median frame P99 was about 0.290 ms worse and its
median FPS was lower (59.257 versus 59.708). The improvement is therefore not
consistent enough to adopt under the strict gate; the result is **NO MATERIAL
DIFFERENCE**, not a renderer correctness failure. M1 is functionally valid but
its frequent bounded-wait timeouts make it a poor low-spec choice. No 4x or 16x
configuration was used or enabled by this audit.

## Remaining physical-gate items

The fixed F2 gameplay scene is covered, including savestate load. A single
continuous 30-minute session, match-end/recap, reset-after-load, resize,
fullscreen, minimize/restore and renderer-switch visual handover remain
unclaimed. The Vulkan validation-layer gate remains blocked by the app's direct
bundled MoltenVK loader; this does not invalidate the capture results above.
